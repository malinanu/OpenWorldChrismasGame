These terms apply to Frostvale, the online Christmas game at openworldchrismasgame.3logiq.com ("the game"). By playing you agree to them.

Accounts

You need an account to play online, keep your progress and buy fight tokens. Keep your password private. One account per person; we may suspend accounts used for cheating, abuse or fraud.

Fight tokens

Fight tokens are a virtual item used only inside the game to enter the Boxing Ring and the Sword Arena. Every new account receives 3 free tokens. Tokens can be bought in packs (Rs 100 for 10, Rs 200 for 22, Rs 500 for 60). Prices are in Sri Lankan rupees and include any applicable taxes.

Tokens have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, sold or moved to another account, and are used up when you enter a fight (whether you win or lose, leave, or lose your connection). The mini-games, snowball fights and practice with the snowman are free.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by PayHere (payhere.lk). We never see or store your card details. Tokens are added to your account as soon as PayHere confirms the payment.
