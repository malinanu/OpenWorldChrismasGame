Refund policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Fight tokens are a digital item delivered to your account immediately after payment.

When we refund

  • You were charged but did not receive your tokens (we check with PayHere and either add the tokens or refund you).
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • You bought tokens by mistake and have not used any of them: ask within 7 days and we will refund the pack.

When we don't

Tokens that have already been used to enter fights cannot be refunded, and nor can tokens on accounts suspended for cheating or abuse.

How to ask

Email us with your username and the order number (it starts with "FV"). Refunds go back to the original payment method through PayHere, usually within 5–10 working days. If a payment is reversed (a chargeback), the tokens from that pack are removed from your account.
