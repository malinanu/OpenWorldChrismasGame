Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What we collect

Your username, email address (or mobile number), your chosen character and language, your game progress and scores, and your fight-token balance and purchases. While you play online, your position in the village is shared with other players in your area.

Payments

When you buy tokens we send your name, email and mobile number to PayHere so they can process the payment. Card and bank details are entered on PayHere's own page; we never receive them. We keep a record of each order (amount, tokens, status and PayHere's payment reference).

How we use it

To run your account, save your progress, show leaderboards (usernames and scores only), prevent cheating and fraud, and answer support questions. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while your account exists. Email us to have your account and data deleted; purchase records may be kept as long as the law requires.

Cookies and storage

The game stores your sign-in and settings in your browser's local storage. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
